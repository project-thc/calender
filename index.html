How to use My Planner

A two-minute tour. Open any topic below.

Quick start

Four steps and you are set up:

  1. In Settings, type your name and edit your subjects and weekly hour targets.
  2. Add your school or study hours as blocks in the Timetable.
  3. Add your next exam in Revision so the countdown starts.
  4. Press N or tap New task to add your first task, then tick it off.
☷Tasks
  • Add a task with the New task button or the N key. Give it a subject, due date, optional time, priority and notes.
  • Repeat makes a task come back every day, on weekdays, or weekly. Each day is ticked separately.
  • Link a task to an exam to see a countdown on it.
  • Tick the box to finish it. Finish them all to see a surprise.
  • Late tasks show Overdue. Tap ↷ to move one to today.
  • Use search, filters and sorting on the Tasks page. Deleted something by mistake? Tap Undo in the pop-up.
▤Calendar
  • Pink dots are tasks, yellow are exams, green are your weekly timetable blocks.
  • Tap a day to see everything planned for it, and add a task for that exact date.
▦Timetable
  • Time blocks repeat every week: classes, work, sport, study time.
  • Use ＋ Add under a day, ✎ to edit and × to remove.
  • Today's blocks and timed tasks appear together in Today's schedule on the Overview, with Now and Next markers.
✎Revision
  • Log study session records minutes for a subject. Progress bars compare them with each subject's weekly target.
  • Set your overall weekly goal in hours on the right.
  • Add exams to get countdowns. Edit or delete them any time.
◷Focus timer
  • Choose what you are working on, pick 25, 45 or 50 minutes, then press Start focus.
  • When a focus session ends it is logged automatically to Revision. Breaks are not logged.
  • The timer keeps counting if you switch tabs or close the page, and allows notifications if you accept the prompt.
♡Journal
  • Daily keeps one note per date. Brain dump is a single free page.
  • Everything autosaves while you type. Use the prompts if you are stuck, and tap a past entry to reopen it.
☑Checklists, dragging and focus links
  • Type one item per line in a task's Checklist box, then tick the items right on the task.
  • On a computer, drag a task onto a day in the Calendar, or onto a day in "Your week at a glance", to move it. On a phone, edit the task and change its date.
  • In the Focus room, link a session to a task. The minutes are added to that task (⏱).
↗Stats
  • The heatmap shows how much you studied each day. Darker means more.
  • The weekly chart splits your minutes by subject. You also get your streaks and your best day and time.
  • Everything comes from the sessions you log, so log (or time) every session.
❀Self-care
  • Check in with your mood, water and sleep each day, and tick your daily habits. Add your own habits.
  • Try the breathing circle when you feel stressed. The cycle tracker is optional and private.
🎧Sounds and reminders
  • In the Focus room pick Rain, Waves or Soft hum and set the volume.
  • In Settings → Daily reminder choose a time. Allow notifications when asked. It only works while the app is open.
🎨Themes, backgrounds and effects
  • In Settings → Appearance choose one of six colour themes and Light, Dark or Match my device.
  • In Backgrounds & effects pick a background (Aurora, Sunset, Dots, Grid, Stripes) and an effect (floating petals, sparkles, bubbles, rain). Choose Plain and None for a quiet look.
📦Backups and privacy
  • Your data stays in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Clearing browser data erases it, so use Export backup now and then and Import backup to restore.
  • Export .ics sends tasks, timetable and exams to Apple, Google or Outlook calendar.
📱Install on your phone
  • iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
  • After the first visit it also works offline.

Settings & backups

Your planner should feel safe, useful and yours.

📦 Back up your planner

Export your tasks, timetable, revision history, exams and notes to a JSON file. Keep a copy somewhere safe.

Importing a backup replaces the planner data currently in this browser. Export first if you need to keep both.

🌷 Your planner, your routine

Change your weekly study goal and manage your routine in the timetable. The app works locally without signing in.

🎨 Appearance

Pick a colour theme and choose how light or dark the planner looks.

✨ Backgrounds & effects

Add a moving or patterned background and floating effects. Pick a colour theme above, then mix and match.

Background
Effect

Effects switch off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion.

📚 Subjects & weekly targets

Rename subjects, set weekly hour targets, or add your own. Your history keeps working.

☁️ Account & sync

Sign in to keep your planner the same on your PC and phone.

🔔 Daily reminder

A gentle nudge with your tasks for the day. It works while My Planner is open in a tab or installed on your phone.

🗓️ Calendar export

Download your tasks, timetable and exams as an .ics file for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. Timed tasks get a 10-minute reminder.

🔒 Privacy & storage

This rebuilt version saves data in this browser's local storage. It does not upload your tasks to a server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see your planner data.

Important: browser storage is not an encrypted vault. Use device security and keep backups. Private browsing, clearing browser data, or switching browsers can remove access to local data.

↻ Start fresh

Remove the current planner data from this browser and return to the starter timetable. This cannot be undone unless you have an exported backup.
